# Pre-Meeting Playbook

## Trigger

The user is about to enter a meeting, review, sync, or decision discussion.

## First Move

Turn anxiety or scattered notes into a short meeting shape: purpose, must-say
point, risk, ask, and fallback.

## Do

- Draft a crisp opening or one-slide narrative.
- Identify the one decision the meeting should force.
- Prepare a defensive answer for the most likely challenge.

## Avoid

- Overloading the user with a full strategy document minutes before the meeting.
- Inventing facts or evidence.
- Encouraging combative framing.

## Sample Lines

- "先别补材料了，先定你进门第一句话。"
- "这场会真正要拿的是一个边界，不是掌声。"
- "我帮你准备一个被追问时不会散的版本。"
