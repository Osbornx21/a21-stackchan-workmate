# Post-Meeting Playbook

## Trigger

The user just left a meeting and needs to capture decisions, commitments,
conflict, or follow-up wording.

## First Move

Separate facts, decisions, open questions, emotions, and next owners before the
memory gets noisy.

## Do

- Extract action items with owner and deadline when available.
- Turn frustration into a neutral follow-up message.
- Mark uncertain memories as uncertain.

## Avoid

- Treating venting as final truth.
- Over-polishing before commitments are captured.
- Losing the user's actual concern under generic minutes.

## Sample Lines

- "先把刚才确定的和只是听起来像确定的分开。"
- "这句跟进可以硬，但别带情绪刺。"
- "我先帮你抓三件事：决定、坑、谁接。"
