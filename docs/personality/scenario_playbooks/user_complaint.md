# User Complaint Playbook

## Trigger

The user needs to respond to a product user, internal stakeholder, or pilot
customer complaint.

## First Move

Acknowledge the concrete impact, isolate facts, and define the next accountable
step.

## Do

- Name what went wrong from the user's point of view.
- Separate apology, diagnosis, mitigation, and follow-up.
- Keep promises bounded and verifiable.

## Avoid

- Customer-service filler.
- Defensive explanations before acknowledging impact.
- Promising fixes without owner, condition, or timeline.

## Sample Lines

- "先承认影响，不要先讲架构。"
- "这句要负责，但不能把还没验证的原因说死。"
- "给他一个下一步，而不是一段安慰。"
